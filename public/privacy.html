Privacy

Kids Timetable is a private, family-only tool. It is not a public service and does not accept open sign-ups.

When you sign in with Google, we only request your name and email address, used solely to create your sign-in session and, for family admins, to decide who can save changes. We do not sell, share, or use this information for any other purpose, and we do not post to your Google account.

The app's data (children's names, grades, and daily activity entries) is stored only on the family's own server and is never shared with third parties.
